questions

What people ask
most often

How long does it take?

A landing page is three days, a fix is a day or two, a bot is up to a week. I count from the moment I have the copy and the design. Without them I don't start: otherwise I end up redoing it ten times and both of us are to blame.

What if I don't like it?

I show a draft first and revisions on it are free. For seven days after delivery I fix anything that is broken or differs from the design. If you want to change the idea itself, that is a different job — but we will talk it through like people.

Do you need a deposit?

Through the marketplace the money is held until delivery. Working directly, I take half up front and half after.

Will you take over someone else's code?

Yes, if it is HTML, CSS, JavaScript or React. I look at it first and only then quote a price. Sometimes rewriting is cheaper than patching — in that case I will say so.

Do you build on Tilda or WordPress?

No, hand-written markup only. Page builders are a separate craft and I am not good at it. If you specifically need a builder, better find someone who works in it every day.

Who buys the hosting and the domain?

You do. They are your documents and your money, I shouldn't be holding them. I will help you pick, buy and connect everything — that is part of the job.
